### Purpose of repository

Code used to analyze the methylation components of snmCT-seq/snm3C-seq in our preprint.

### Citation

Li TW\*, Langerman J\*, Liu C\*, Sun Y\*, et al. Epigenomic heterogeneity and spatial organization during human reprogramming. *bioRxiv* (2026). [https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.15.757424](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.15.757424v1)

\*These authors contributed equally.

### Versions
- ALLCools 1.0.5 [https://lhqing.github.io/ALLCools/intro.html](https://lhqing.github.io/ALLCools/intro.html) - used for handling methylation files, including generation of CpG-level methylation estimates, dimensionality reduction, and pseudobulk aggregation
- leidenalg 0.10.2 [https://github.com/vtraag/leidenalg](https://github.com/vtraag/leidenalg)
- Harmony 0.0.10 [https://github.com/immunogenomics/harmony](https://github.com/immunogenomics/harmony)
- Scanpy 1.10.0 [https://github.com/scverse/scanpy](https://github.com/scverse/scanpy) - used to call DEGs in specific contexts - Start vs. Fail1, IPS-SE vs. IPS-LE
